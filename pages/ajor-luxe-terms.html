Ajor Luxe Terms and Conditions

Effective from 1 april 2022

Introduction

These Terms and Conditions are an understanding between Ajor Interio, hereinafter referred to as the Company, and its Customers.

The Company is engaged in the business of providing end-to-end interior designing solutions under its several brands including Ajor Interio and Ajor Luxe.

Ajor Luxe is a brand of the Company which caters to providing high-end interior services to its end Customers. Under Ajor Luxe, the Company provides industry expert designers, premium catalogue, high quality installation, dedicated POC at all times, and an overall premium experience for the Customer.

Customer refers to an individual, organization or entity that purchases goods or services from Ajor Luxe.

Design Consultation Package

The Company has provided a Design Consultation Package to the Customer, which includes Design Philosophy, Design Theme, Space Programming, Bubble diagram, Moodboards, rendering a part of the Customer's dream space as part of the design consultation presentation, and providing an estimated quotation of the project.

Design Consultation Charges

The Customer has paid an amount of INR 25,000 as Design Consultation Charges, which is a non-refundable fee. The Design Consultation Charges shall, however, be adjusted and deducted from the First Installment Payment, as defined hereunder.

Design and Convenience Fee

For all Ajor Luxe projects, the Customer shall be charged a Design and Service Convenience Fee of eight percent (8%) on the final quote value of the project.

Price and Payment Terms

The exact price of the Customer project will depend on measurements, scope of work and changes in designs, material and finishes. Based on these revisions, Customers can expect the quote value to rise or drop by approximately 5-10%. However, in case of non-standard product selection, customization, or additional scope of work, prices may substantially increase from the initial quote value.

Based on the Customer's initial discussion with Ajor Interio representatives, the Customer shall make an initial payment of 10% of quote value for booking the order with Ajor Interio, subject to the terms and conditions mentioned herein, if applicable.

The total quote value of the project may have both woodwork and non-woodwork, including civil work, custom carpentry, appliances, and HDS - Home Decor Services. Payment towards the same shall be as per the clauses below.

The price structure has 4 installments:

  1. First Installment: 10% of the total quote value, woodwork plus non-woodwork, paid at the time of order booking.
  2. Second Installment: 10% of the total quote value, woodwork plus non-woodwork, payable before Site Masking. The Customer order shall move to the site masking and design finalization stage only after payment of the Second Installment.
  3. Third Installment: 30% of the woodwork quote value and 80% of the non-woodwork quote value, payable at the time of signing of the Works Contract. The Customer order shall move to the production stage only after payment of the Third Installment.
  4. Fourth or Final Installment: Remaining 50% of the woodwork quote value, payable as per the due date mentioned in the Ready to Dispatch email sent to the Customer.

Note: A non-refundable convenience fee of 2%, including GST, on payment amount may be levied on certain modes of payment including credit card, debit card, net banking, wallet and other modes.

The name used for order booking should be in line with the KYC of the Customer. No name change or GST number inclusion is possible once an order is booked. Disclosing complete names at the time of order booking will help for all legal communications, availing finance options, GST invoice in case of registered person, and processing refund in case of cancellation as specified in these Terms and Conditions.

For booking an order with Ajor Luxe, the minimum final order or quote value should amount to INR 12,00,000.

The initial quote value provided by Ajor Interio at the time of order booking may not include civil, plumbing, gas-piping or electrical work. The exact value of the quote will depend on the site conditions.

The fourth or final installment payment has to be completed before material dispatch from the factory and not before installation.

Between First Installment and Third Installment payment stage, brand partnered categories such as appliances, lights and loose furniture are subject to price fluctuation and may not be available due to stock issues from the manufacturer's end. In such cases, the Customer will have an option to choose other available products.

We have categorised the work of Ajor Interio as interior solutions and considered it as Works Contract Services of Interior Solutions, SAC 995476. Place of supply will be considered based on the state where the installation is to be carried out irrespective of bill-to state. GST will be collected on each advance payment and GST invoices are raised upon completion of the project.

Cash Payments

If a Customer wishes to make payments in cash, Ajor Interio can accept cash up to Rs. 1,99,999 between all stages of the respective project.

Maximum amount of cash that Ajor Interio accepts without PAN is Rs. 49,999.

Maximum amount of cash that Ajor Interio accepts with PAN is Rs. 1,99,999. PAN should belong to the Customer only, and it is mandatory to obtain a copy of the PAN from the Customer and share it with the Finance team to approve payments.

Orders cannot be split to accommodate additional cash from a Customer.

Payment Delay and Quote Validity

If the fourth installment, i.e. the final 50% of the estimate, is not paid by the Customer within the time stipulated in the Ready to Dispatch email sent to the Customer, the Customer shall be liable to pay a demurrage charge of INR 500 per day, from the second day that the installment becomes due until the amount is paid in full.

The final quote value shall be valid for a period of six (6) months. In case of extension of quote validity beyond 6 months, prices shall vary as per the then existing pricing.

All payments made by the Customer throughout their engagement with Ajor Interio should be approved by Ajor Interio via email communication. Only on such approval shall the payment be deemed effective.

Installation Checks

When civil work is done at site, including false ceiling, electrical, plumbing, gas piping, wall demolition or wall partition, hand marks and scratches are bound to occur and cannot be avoided. It is recommended to have a final coat of paint after completion of all interiors.

Delivery and Installation

The delivery and installation of the Customer's order shall be on or before the delivery date prescribed in the quotation, and at the address given by the Customer.

Delivery Date will be calculated from the date of completion of the following requirements, referred to as All-Set-Go:

  1. Both parties, Customer and Ajor Interio, signing off on the designs and specifications for the home interiors.
  2. Customer making all payments strictly in accordance with the specified payment milestones. The Delivery Guarantee will commence from the date of approval of payment, which shall be confirmed via email by Ajor Interio.
  3. Customer handing over the Site to Ajor Interio in a condition that meets the requirements of Customer's Site Readiness Checklist as per the Works Contract.

Note: Back panel shall be PreLam MDF for all carcasses in any core material other than BWP, Boiling Water Proof Ply. For all carcasses with BWP Ply as core material, the back panel, wherever applicable, is to be Ply.
