Terms and Conditions

By participating in the Ajor Interio Refer & Earn program, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

  1. Eligibility: The program is open to all existing Ajor Interio customers with an active or completed Ajor Interio project.
  2. Successful Referral: A referral is considered successful when:
    • The referred customer places an order with Ajor Interio.
    • The referred customer pays the first installment of the total project value.
    • The referred customer project includes a minimum value of INR 4,00,000, maintained across all project stages.

    Note: Customers already existing in the Ajor Interio database through any source of lead generation are not eligible to be counted as referrals.

  3. Referral Bonus Structure: The referral bonus will be provided in the manner as follows:
    • 1st successful referral - Premium Home Décor Gift
    • 2nd successful referral - Special Luxury Benefits & Surprise Gifts
    • 3rd successful referral onwards - INR 5,000 Reward
  4. Referral Bonus Payment: The payment of the referral bonus shall be credited directly to the bank account of the existing customer.
  5. General Terms:
    • Referrals must be genuine. Ajor Interio reserves the right to deny bonuses in cases of suspected fraud or misuse.
    • Ajor Interio may, at its sole discretion, modify, suspend, or terminate the referral program at any time without prior notice.
    • Referral bonuses are non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash except as per the conditions stated above.
    • The referral bonus shall be subject to TDS, as per the Income Tax Act.
  6. Contact Us: For any queries regarding the program, please contact ajorinterio@gmail.com.

Note: These Terms and Conditions apply to all customers making a successful referral effective from 1st March, 2025.
