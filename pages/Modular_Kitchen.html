FAQs on Modular Kitchen Design

What is a modular kitchen?

A modular kitchen is built using factory-made cabinet modules, shutters, hardware, accessories and finishes that are assembled on site for a clean, functional kitchen.

What is the best approach to kitchen design?

Start with your cooking habits, storage needs, appliance list and available space. A good design balances workflow, ventilation, durability, budget and easy maintenance.

What is the difference between a modular kitchen, a semi-modular kitchen, and a non-modular kitchen?

A modular kitchen uses complete factory-made modules. A semi-modular kitchen works around existing civil counters or walls. A non-modular kitchen is usually carpenter-built on site with less standardisation.

What are the benefits of installing a modular kitchen?

It offers better storage, consistent finish quality, quicker installation, easier repairs, planned accessories, and a more polished look than traditional on-site work.

What layouts are available for modular kitchens, and which ones are the most popular?

Popular layouts include straight, L-shaped, parallel, U-shaped, island and peninsula kitchens. L-shaped and parallel kitchens are common because they suit many Indian homes.

What materials are used in modular kitchen interiors?

Common materials include plywood, MDF, HDF, particle board, laminates, acrylic, membrane, PU, glass, hardware channels, hinges and storage accessories.

What is the average cost of a modular kitchen in India?

The cost depends on size, material, finish, hardware, accessories and appliances. A basic kitchen can start from a modest budget, while premium finishes and storage systems increase the price.

What are the trending modular kitchen designs for 2026 that I should explore?

Trends include handleless shutters, warm neutrals, fluted glass, matte finishes, hidden storage, tall pantry units, integrated lighting and smart accessories.

What are the popular styles for modular kitchens?

Popular styles include modern, minimalist, classic, Scandinavian, industrial, contemporary Indian and luxury high-gloss kitchens.

What are the common modules in a modular kitchen?

Common modules include base cabinets, wall cabinets, tall units, sink units, hob units, drawer units, corner units, pantry units and appliance units.

What are the countertop options available for modular kitchens, and how do I choose the right one?

Granite, quartz, solid surface, marble and compact surfaces are common. Choose based on stain resistance, heat resistance, maintenance, budget and the look you want.

I have already installed granite in my kitchen. Can I still get a modular kitchen done?

Yes. A semi-modular setup can be designed around existing granite counters, depending on site measurements and structural condition.

Which colour schemes work best for modular kitchens to create the ideal look?

Whites, greys, wood tones, sage, beige, navy and two-tone combinations work well. Pick colours based on natural light, floor colour and the rest of your home.

How do I select finishes for my modular kitchen?

Choose finishes by comparing durability, cleaning effort, fingerprints, moisture exposure, budget and style. Laminate is practical, while acrylic, PU and glass create a premium look.

Can modular kitchens include smart storage and convenient accessories?

Yes. Pull-outs, tandem drawers, tall units, corner units, wicker baskets, bins, cutlery trays and dish racks can be integrated into the design.

Can I see a visualised version of my modular kitchen before placing an order?

Yes. A design consultation can include layout planning and visualisation so you can review the kitchen before finalising.

What add-ons may influence the price of my modular kitchen?

Accessories, premium hardware, appliances, lighting, tall units, corner systems, chimney, hob, sink, countertop and special finishes can affect the final cost.

Are there budget-friendly modular kitchen options available?

Yes. You can manage cost with practical layouts, laminate finishes, standard hardware and selective accessories.

Is Ajor Interio available in my city for modular kitchen installation?

Contact Ajor Interio with your location to confirm service availability and schedule a consultation.

How much maintenance does a modular kitchen require to stay in top condition?

Regular wiping, quick spill cleanup, dry cabinets, proper ventilation and periodic hardware checks are usually enough for routine maintenance.

What are the best cleaning practices to maintain modular kitchen surfaces?

Use a soft cloth and mild cleaner. Avoid harsh abrasives, excess water, strong chemicals and sharp scraping on shutters or countertops.

What kind of quality checks are carried out during installation?

Checks usually cover measurements, cabinet alignment, shutter gaps, hardware movement, finish quality, countertop fit, accessory operation and final cleanup.

How long does it take to design and install a modular kitchen from start to finish?

Timelines depend on design finalisation, site readiness, material selection and production. Your designer can confirm a project-specific schedule.

What is the process for designing and installing a modular kitchen?

The process usually includes consultation, site measurement, design, quotation, material selection, production, delivery, installation and quality check.

The design is based on your measurements, cooking style, appliance needs, storage requirements, budget and preferred finishes.

How is a modular kitchen different from a carpenter-made kitchen?

Modular kitchens are factory-made with standard modules and consistent finishes. Carpenter-made kitchens are built on site and depend heavily on local workmanship.

What is the average lifespan of an Ajor Interio kitchen?

Lifespan depends on material, usage and maintenance. With good materials and care, a modular kitchen can last for many years.

What factors matter when choosing an interior partner?

Look at design quality, material transparency, warranty, installation process, service support, reviews, timelines and cost clarity.

Can Ajor Interio convert my closed kitchen into an open island?

It may be possible if the structure, plumbing, electrical points and ventilation allow it. A site check is needed before confirming.

Can you integrate waste segregation?

Yes. Pull-out bins and separate dry/wet waste systems can be included in the base cabinet design.

Where do heavy tawas and sil-battas go?

Heavy cookware should be stored in strong base drawers or dedicated lower cabinets with suitable load-bearing hardware.

Warranty coverage varies by material, hardware and package. Review the warranty document for covered components, exclusions and service terms.

Can I relocate my kitchen when I shift homes?

Some modular components can be dismantled and reused, but relocation depends on the new kitchen size, wall layout and service points.

Are your materials eco-friendly?

Material options vary. Ask for eco-conscious boards, finishes and low-emission products during selection.

How fire-safe is an Ajor Interio kitchen?

Fire safety depends on appliance placement, electrical planning, chimney use, material choices and proper installation around heat zones.

What is the best hob-plus-chimney combo for heavy tadkas?

Choose a powerful chimney with suitable suction, easy-clean filters and a hob size that matches your cooking style. The exact combo depends on kitchen size and ventilation.
